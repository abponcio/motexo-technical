# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: “use best frameworks, best tools for motion”. React, TypeScript, Vite and Motion provide the interactive marketing experience with static hosting. No server or database is needed for browsing and preparing an enquiry.

## Users

Prospective customers considering electrical, IT/network infrastructure and practical office, warehouse or event work in Dubai. Office and facilities decision-makers are a working audience inference from the supplied brief, not a verified customer study.

## Product Purpose

Show the quality and care of Motexo's technical workmanship, help visitors explore relevant services and real work imagery, and prepare an enquiry.

## Positioning

The user's firsthand observation: clean, carefully organized wiring is a particular strength. The interface should make that detail visible.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Service explorer, cable-detail interaction, social-work gallery, responsive navigation and a reviewable enquiry draft. Do not claim a draft has been sent. Exact sending integration is not yet verified. No accounts, CRM or persistent personal data.

## Brand Commitments

Use the official bilingual red logo extracted from the supplied business document. Preserve actual social photographs and their context. The user requested reference screens before implementation, reviewed the resulting direction and then said “okay lets go and try to make one”. Carry that direction forward; no new identity selection is needed.

## Evidence on Hand

The sibling motexo-reference-screens folder holds the full homepage, expertise, work, enquiry, mobile and motion references. social-assets contains the unchanged original logo and four public Instagram work collages. Some collages include architectural renderings. The electrical/network/office/warehouse/event strengths and tennis work were supplied by the user; precise contracts, certifications, project totals and performance outcomes have not been independently established.

Only the logo from the quotation is authorized design content. Keep financial, commercial, signature and stamp material out of the website and generation prompts. Generated rack and electrical photographs are illustrative and must be identified accordingly.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate workmanship through useful detail.
- Preserve the existing branding and actual source evidence.
- Make navigation and enquiry straightforward on mobile and keyboard.
- Use purposeful, finite motion with a reduced-motion path.
- Never invent commercial proof or imply a message was sent.
